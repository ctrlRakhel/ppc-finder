A COLLECTOR'S NOTE
✉

A living postal heritage.

Permanent Pictorial Cancellations (PPCs) are special postal cancellations used at designated post offices. Their designs depict monuments, heritage sites, wildlife, cultural traditions and other subjects of local significance.

PPC Compass helps collectors find post offices associated with permanent pictorial cancellations across India, calculate nearby distances and get directions.

A recorded introduction date does not guarantee that the cancellation device is currently available at the counter.

BEFORE YOU VISIT
  1. Check the post office and PIN code.
  2. Confirm the PPC device is currently available.
  3. Ask about counter hours and postage requirements.
  4. Carry stamped covers or postcards as appropriate.
